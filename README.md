# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analytics

## 📊 Turning Customer Data into Actionable Business Insights

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to uncover patterns in **customer spending, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, customer loyalty, shipping preferences, ratings, and revenue contribution**.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence workflow**, starting from raw customer data and progressing through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL analysis, and interactive Power BI visualization.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to understand:

* Who the customers are
* What products customers purchase
* Which categories generate the most revenue
* How customers respond to discounts
* Whether subscribers spend differently from non-subscribers
* Which products receive the highest ratings
* How shipping type relates to purchase amount
* How customers can be segmented based on previous purchases
* Which products perform best within each category
* Which age groups contribute the most revenue

The analysis converts raw customer transaction data into **business-focused insights and recommendations**.

# 🧹 Data Preparation

The Python notebook is used to inspect and prepare the dataset before performing the business analysis.

The preprocessing workflow includes:

### Missing Value Handling

Missing values are identified and handled during data preparation.

### Column Standardization

Column names are standardized to make them easier to work with in Python and SQL.

For example:

```text
purchase_amount_(usd)
```

is standardized to:

```text
purchase_amount
```

### Age Group Creation

Customers are categorized into age groups to support demographic analysis.

### Purchase Frequency Transformation

Purchase-frequency values are transformed into approximate purchase intervals.

Examples include:

```text
Weekly       → 7 days
Fortnightly  → 14 days
Monthly      → 30 days
Quarterly    → 90 days
Annually     → 365 days
```

### Feature Preparation

Additional analytical fields are created to support customer segmentation and business analysis.

# 🔍 SQL Business Analysis

The SQL analysis answers 10 important business questions.

## 1. Revenue by Gender

**Question:**

> What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?

The analysis uses purchase amount aggregated by gender.

---

## 2. Discounted Purchases Above Average

**Question:**

> Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?

This identifies customers who received discounts while spending above the overall average.

---

## 3. Highest-Rated Products

**Question:**

> Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?

The SQL groups products by average review rating and ranks the top five.

---

## 4. Shipping Type Comparison

**Question:**

> How does average purchase amount differ between Standard and Express shipping?

The analysis compares average purchase amount for Standard and Express shipping.

---

## 5. Subscriber vs. Non-Subscriber Spending

**Question:**

> Do subscribed customers spend more?

The analysis compares:

* Number of customers
* Average spending
* Total revenue

between subscribers and non-subscribers.

---

## 6. Discount Usage by Product

**Question:**

> Which five products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?

The analysis calculates the percentage of purchases where a discount was applied for each product.

---

## 7. Customer Segmentation

Customers are segmented according to previous purchases:

| Segment      | Previous Purchases |
| ------------ | -----------------: |
| 🆕 New       |                  1 |
| 🔄 Returning |               2–10 |
| ⭐ Loyal      |       More than 10 |

This segmentation logic is implemented using a SQL `CASE` statement.

---

## 8. Top Products by Category

**Question:**

> What are the top three most purchased products within each category?

A SQL CTE and `ROW_NUMBER()` window function are used to rank products within each category.

---

## 9. Repeat Buyers and Subscription

**Question:**

> Are customers with more than five previous purchases also likely to subscribe?

The analysis compares subscription status among repeat buyers.

---

## 10. Revenue by Age Group

**Question:**

> What is the revenue contribution of each age group?

Revenue is aggregated by age group and ranked from highest to lowest.
